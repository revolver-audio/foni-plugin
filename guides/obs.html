How to use FONI in OBS Studio

FONI keeps your voice at one steady level on stream: quiet words come up, shouts come down, harsh S sounds are softened and the output never clips. Here is how to put it on your microphone in OBS in about two minutes.

Why you need a small helper

OBS Studio can only load older VST2 plug-ins by itself. FONI is a modern VST3 plug-in, so OBS needs a free VST3 host: atkAudio Plugin for OBS (Windows, macOS and Linux, OBS 31.1.1 or later). It is a separate free project by atkAudio, not made by us.

Windows: one installer

  1. Download and run the FONI installer (0.9.5 or later).
  2. If OBS Studio is installed, the installer offers OBS Studio support. Leave it ticked: it installs FONI and the atkAudio host into OBS's plug-in folder for you. (If you already have atkAudio, the option is hidden and your copy is left alone.)
  3. Restart OBS, then follow the steps under Load FONI on your mic.

macOS

  1. Install FONI with the macOS installer from the download section.
  2. Install atkAudio from its OBS forum page (the macOS .pkg), then restart OBS.

Load FONI on your mic

  1. In the Audio Mixer, click the three dots next to your microphone and choose Filters.
  2. Click + and choose atkAudio Plugin Host. Name it FONI.
  3. Click Open Plugin Settings. The first time, choose Options → Scan for new or updated VST3 plugins and click Scan.
  4. Pick FONI (by Revolver Audio) from the list and load it. The FONI window opens.
  5. Choose the preset Live Stream. It aims at −14 LUFS, the loudness YouTube and Twitch viewers are used to.
  6. Talk for a few seconds and watch FONI's meter settle. Close the window: the settings are saved with your OBS scene collection.
Put FONI last. If you also use OBS's own filters on the mic (noise suppression, gain), keep FONI at the bottom of the filter list so it sets the final level. Remove OBS's Compressor and Limiter filters: FONI already does both, and two compressors make the voice pump.

Delay and lip sync

FONI looks a few milliseconds ahead so it can catch peaks before they clip, and its noise cleanup works in short blocks. Together that delays your voice by about 25 ms at 48 kHz, less than one video frame at 30 fps, so lip sync is normally fine. If your voice ever looks late against your webcam, add OBS's Render Delay filter to the camera source and set it to about 25 ms.

No OBS plug-ins allowed? Use the FONI app

FONI also comes as a standalone app. Choose your microphone as its input and a virtual audio cable (for example the free VB-CABLE) as its output, then select that cable as the microphone in OBS, Discord or Zoom. Plug-in mode inside OBS is simpler, so use this only if you need FONI in several apps at once.
