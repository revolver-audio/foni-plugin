When Auphonic is the better choice

If you want one upload that also gives you a transcript, chapter marks and a published episode, or you edit on a phone or tablet with no DAW, Auphonic is hard to beat. Its multitrack mode is also excellent for interviews recorded on separate tracks.

When FONI is the better choice

  • You publish more than two hours a month and don't want a subscription.
  • You already edit in a DAW and want the finished sound while you edit, not after an upload.
  • You record audiobooks and want to see RMS, peak and noise floor against the ACX limits as you go.
  • You stream or record live and need the processing in real time.
  • Your audio can't leave your computer (client work, NDAs).
Honest limits. FONI is a free public beta from a small studio (Revolver Audio, Greece). It is a single-voice processor: it does not edit out mistakes, transcribe, or publish. The installers are not code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS show a warning on first install (step-by-step help).

Getting the "Auphonic sound" with FONI in three steps

  1. Insert FONI on your voice track (last in the chain, or right before your final limiter).
  2. Pick a preset: Podcast - Natural for most shows, Audiobook (ACX-style) for narration.
  3. Choose the platform under Target (Apple Podcasts −16, YouTube/Spotify −14) and set Amount to taste. The user guide explains every control.
